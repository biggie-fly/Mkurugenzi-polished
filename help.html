Delivery

Orders placed before 2pm on a working day leave the same day. You'll get an SMS with tracking once your parcel is on its way.

Exchanges

If something doesn't fit, you can exchange it for another size or colour within 7 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed and have their tags on.

To start an exchange, message us on WhatsApp or use the contact form with your order number.

Payments

We accept M-Pesa, Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal. For M-Pesa, you'll get a prompt on your phone to enter your PIN. Card payments are processed securely and we never store your card number.

    Questions people ask

    How do I know my order went through?
    You'll see an order number on screen and get a confirmation by email and SMS.
    Can I change my order after paying?
    Yes, if it hasn't left our store. Message us on WhatsApp with your order number as soon as possible.
    Do you deliver outside Kenya?
    Not yet. Message us if you're abroad and we'll see what we can arrange.
    Do you do bulk or corporate orders?
    Yes. Use the contact form and choose "Bulk or corporate order".
    My M-Pesa prompt didn't arrive. What now?
    Check that your phone is on and the number is correct, then tap "Resend prompt" at checkout. If it still doesn't come, choose another payment method or contact us.
