Our story

Mkurugenzi is Swahili for director. The one who runs things.

More than just a brand.

We started Mkurugenzi in Nairobi to make clothes for people who are building something: a business, a career, a name. Clean cuts, heavy fabrics and colours that go with everything.

Every collection, from Black Excellence to Botanical, is designed here at home. We keep the range tight so each piece earns its place in your wardrobe.
